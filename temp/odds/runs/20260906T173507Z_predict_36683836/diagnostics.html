Which inputs the trees used

Gain is the loss reduction each input bought when a tree split on it, as a share of the total. The top ten inputs account for 14% of it, and 3 of 126 inputs were never split on at all. An input with no gain costs nothing but adds a chance for the search to fit noise, so a long idle tail is a reason to narrow the feature set.

SHAP: which direction, and how consistently

One dot per match, for the 250 most recent training rows. Position is the input's contribution to the home-win log odds for that match; colour is the input's own value, low to high. A clean colour split either side of zero is a consistent effect.
